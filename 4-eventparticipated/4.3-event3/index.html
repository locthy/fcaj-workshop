Event 3

Event Report: “AWS Hackathon Product Showcase & Workshop”

Event Purpose

  • Listen and learn from real-world products developed by competing teams within the AWS hackathon framework.
  • Gather practical experience from the product development process under time pressure (24 hours).
  • Explore AI-integrated solutions (such as Amazon Bedrock, Agentic AI) and modern cloud architecture models.

Featured Projects Presented

  • S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D (Team 3KA): An intelligent crowd density evaluation, prediction, and hazard detection system utilizing YOLO + ByteTrack, Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Bedrock Agent, and a React Dashboard.
  • KFC Bot Agent / Multi-Channel AI Agent (One Team): A multi-channel conversational ordering assistant on Zalo and WhatsApp integrated with Amazon Bedrock and the AgentCore architecture to optimize operations and costs.
  • Solution Architect Professional AI Native App (Plan V): An AI application that analyzes requirement documents (BRD/PRD), automatically proposes hybrid-cloud architectures, generates editable Draw.io/AWS diagrams, and estimates cloud costs.
  • Signal Scout (Dream AI Team): An AI platform for analyzing corporate restructuring signals, collecting public data, and aggregating risk reports for management.

Key Highlights & Acquired Skills

Product Development Under Time Pressure (Hackathon Mindset)

  • Scope it tiny: Focus on a core feature and execute it completely rather than biting off more than you can chew with massive, unfinished ideas.
  • Risk management & role allocation: Clearly define each member’s role (coding, design, pitching) and prepare starter toolkits/templates before entering the competition.
  • Overcoming adversity: Practical troubleshooting skills for handling code bugs, sleep deprivation, and time constraints during 24 hours of building.

Real-World AI & Cloud Technology Application

  • Agentic AI & LLM Integration: Understand how to apply Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore, and Knowledge Bases to build AI agents capable of planning, using tools, and taking autonomous actions.
  • Cost-optimized architecture design: Learn how to balance performance and infrastructure costs when deploying AI systems on AWS (optimizing token usage, leveraging Lambda, DynamoDB, and ECS Fargate efficiently).
  • Business process automation: Apply AI to requirement extraction, automatic generation of standard AWS architecture diagrams, and cost estimations from text documents.

Key Learnings

Practical Experience

  • Showing up is half the battle: Daring to participate and take action builds practical experience much faster than merely studying theory.
  • Small, finished work beats big, broken ideas: The true value of a functional MVP (Minimum Viable Product) delivered under pressure.
  • Human connections: The importance of finding teammates with complementary skills and expanding networks with the community and mentors.

Technical Mindset

  • Grasp the trend of integrating AI agents into real-world applications to solve specific business problems (such as automated ordering, corporate risk analysis, and solution architecture assistance).
  • Recognize the importance of preparing a clear demo structure and telling a concise product story within 3 minutes.

Application to Work & Studies

  • Apply the Agentic AI model: Integrate AI agent and Bedrock concepts into upcoming personal projects or assignments.
  • Optimize teamwork workflows: Apply lessons on task division, time management, and rapid MVP creation when participating in competitions or tech projects.
  • Enhance Cloud skills: Proactively dive deeper into architecture design, cost optimization, and managed AWS services learned from the competing teams.

Event Experience

Attending the hackathon product showcase workshop was an eye-opening experience, allowing me to witness firsthand the innovative solutions brought to life by competing teams within a short timeframe:

Real-World Perspectives from Products

  • Observed how teams transformed complex ideas into complete MVP products powered by cutting-edge AWS and AI technologies.
  • Learned from hard-earned lessons, technical challenges, and practical experiences that groups encountered during the 24-hour hackathon.

Positive Community Energy

  • Felt the passion, creativity, and relentless drive of young developers.
  • Broadened perspectives on solving business problems through technology using real case studies from the competition.

Key Takeaways

  • A hackathon is not merely a competition for prizes, but the ultimate environment to push personal limits, master working under pressure, and turn ideas into reality.

Event Photos

Overall, the event provided me with invaluable insights into product development workflows, effective AI/Cloud application strategies, and powerful inspiration to confidently take on future technology challenges."
