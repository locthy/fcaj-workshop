### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is dynamic and open. Everyone actively supports and helps each other. The workspace is spacious and comfortable, though seating capacity can be somewhat limited relative to the number of participating students.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The Admin team is incredibly proactive and supportive whenever questions arise or issues are encountered throughout the learning and working process.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
Although the program does not directly align with my major in Computer Engineering, it allowed me to discover numerous technologies that I can directly apply to my embedded and IoT projects.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
I had a period of focused learning to dive deep into and thoroughly research AWS's extensive service ecosystem. In particular, the practical sharing sessions hosted by experienced seniors provided valuable insights into real-world engineering challenges, system design thinking, and career progression paths within Cloud & AI.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
FCAJ effectively promotes a positive culture of mutual support. Every peer around us can act as both a mentor and a teammate on this FCAJ journey, so you can easily seek assistance from anyone without feeling restricted to formal mentors.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provided an excellent foundation for accessing free AWS resources through a $200 credit upon account setup, detailed guidance documentation, and a collaborative work environment with experienced professionals.

### Additional Questions
- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?  
The $200 initial AWS account credit provided right at the start.

- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?  
The program could consider providing a curated list of sample project topics for students to reference and build upon.

- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?  
Definitely Yes. This program offers an invaluable opportunity for any IT student to significantly expand their technical depth and knowledge base.
